The limited Corporate Guidance service provides high-level procedural and institutional assistance designed to guide entrepreneurs and corporations in the correct formalization and structuring of their business documents in Florida. It is of utmost importance to clarify that this service focuses strictly on aspects of public trust compliance and formal filing, and under no circumstances replaces or constitutes legal advice reserved exclusively for licensed attorneys (Legal Counsel). We position ourselves as the ideal operational bridge to ensure the formal legality of your business structure.

Our assistance covers the preparation, formal structuring, and notarization of essential organizational and operating documents for limited liability companies (LLCs) and traditional corporations. This includes the drafting and notarization of operating agreements (Operating Agreements) that define the ownership percentage and internal operations of the partners; company bylaws or regulations (Bylaws); formal minutes of shareholder and board of directors meetings; and the notary certification of stock registry books and share certificates of the entity.

The correct instrumentation and notary certification of corporate documents is of vital importance to shield the legal security of the company against third parties. Most banking institutions in the United States require notarized and certified copies of Operating Agreements and board resolutions before authorizing the opening of high-volume commercial bank accounts. Likewise, investors, investment funds, and corporate creditors require confirmation that all the company's registry and stock certificates strictly comply with applicable state laws.

At Su Notaria USA, we coordinate and supervise compliance with all your procedures before the Division of Corporations of the Florida Department of State (official Sunbiz platform), avoiding administrative delays due to formal presentation errors. Backed by the extensive business track record of JPX International in structuring businesses in Miami, Gladys Rodríguez will provide you with impeccable and customized guidance so that your corporation strictly complies with the regulations of annual reports filing, corporate amendments, and notarizations of national and international commercial agreements.
