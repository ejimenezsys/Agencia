{% if lang == 'en' %} Unlike in other countries, a notary public in the United States is a state-regulated public trust officer whose primary purpose is to provide formal authenticity to signatures and declarations. They are key for real estate transactions, international powers of attorney, apostilles, trusts, and legal consent forms. {% else %} A diferencia de otros países, el notario en Estados Unidos es un oficial de fe pública regulado a nivel estatal cuyo fin primordial es dar autenticidad formal a firmas y declaraciones.
